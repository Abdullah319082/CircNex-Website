A Second Life for Usable Technology

CircNex purchases eligible retired IT equipment from businesses and organizations, subject to assessment and agreed purchase terms. Suitable devices can be repaired, refurbished or resold for continued use.

For equipment that cannot be reused, we coordinate recycling and any agreed data destruction through certified partners. CircNex does not hold these specialized processing certifications directly; the partner services and documentation are defined for each project.
