04 / GOOD TO KNOW

Common
questions.

What types of organizations can you help? +

We are building services for businesses and organizations that need help with IT deployment, on-site support, device retirement and related logistics. Contact us to discuss a specific project.

Can you handle both installation and equipment removal? +

That's the core idea behind CircNex: support technology as it arrives, changes and eventually leaves the workplace. Exact project scope is agreed case by case.

Do you provide certified data destruction? +

Specific data sanitization or destruction requirements must be discussed before a project. We do not claim a certification or guarantee a method that has not been confirmed in writing.
