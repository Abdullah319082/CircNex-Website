OUR STORY

Practical IT services.
A connected vision.

CircNex Technologies is a developing technology-services business with a mission to support organizations through every transition in their equipment lifecycle.

WHAT CIRCNEX STANDS FOR

From the first install
to the final handoff.

We began with a simple observation: organizations need practical help at both ends of technology ownership. The same organization deploying new workstations may also be replacing hardware, repurposing equipment or closing an office.

CircNex connects these stages with an approachable, technically informed process. We also assist MSPs and IT vendors that need a local technician at a customer site. The name reflects circular thinking and what comes next for technology.
