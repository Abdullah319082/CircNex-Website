FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good questions.
Straight answers.

A practical guide to our services, project scoping and security expectations.

What types of work does CircNex focus on?+

CircNex handles IT equipment deployment and workstation setup, refurbishment and repair, IT asset retirement and recycling coordination, and network cabling installation and decommissioning—including cable runs, patch panels, and wall jacks. Each project is scoped individually.

Do you provide field technicians for MSPs and IT vendors?+

Yes. CircNex can provide qualified local technicians to work at your customer site on your behalf in the DMV area. We support one-time dispatches and recurring contract-based work orders, following an agreed scope and reporting requirements. Availability and terms are confirmed before accepting an assignment.

Do you accept projects from businesses and organizations?+

Yes. CircNex works with businesses and organizations of all sizes, from small offices to multi-site teams. Contact us with the size, location and equipment involved, and we’ll review your project and confirm the scope and next steps.

Which geographic areas do you serve?+

We serve the DMV area. If your project is outside it, tell us where the work would take place and we’ll confirm availability before proposing any service.

Will CircNex erase data from our old devices?+

Yes, when data erasure is part of the agreed scope. Every project is different, so before we take responsibility for any device that stores data, we’ll agree with you in writing on the erasure method, who will perform it, and what documentation you’ll receive. We don’t assume a specific method or certificate unless it’s part of that agreement.

Do you refurbish equipment rather than recycling everything?+

Where practical, we aim to assess condition and potential reuse before selecting an appropriate next step. Feasibility depends on device type, condition, economics and customer requirements.

Can you help with a new office or workstation refresh?+

Our combined IT Equipment Deployment & Workstation Setup service includes staging, monitor and docking station installation, peripheral connections, hardware swaps, and cable organization, subject to the agreed scope.

How can I get an estimate?+

Contact CircNex with the site location, equipment details, timing and work required. For vendor dispatches, include the customer-site requirements and expected reporting. We'll review the scope before discussing an estimate.
