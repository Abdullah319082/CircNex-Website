FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good questions.
Straight answers.

A practical guide to our services, project scoping and security expectations.

What types of work does CircNex focus on?+

CircNex handles equipment deployment and workstation setup, refurbishment and repair, IT asset retirement and recycling coordination, and cable removal and decommissioning. Each project is scoped individually.

Do you accept projects from businesses and organizations?+

Yes. CircNex works with businesses and organizations of all sizes, from small offices to multi-site teams. Contact us with the size, location and equipment involved, and we’ll review your project and confirm the scope and next steps.

Which geographic areas do you serve?+

We serve the DMV area. If your project is outside it, tell us where the work would take place and we’ll confirm availability before proposing any service.

Do you hold R2, e-Stewards, NAID AAA or similar certifications?+

We are not currently advertising company-held certifications. For work that requires specialized certified recycling or data destruction, we can discuss sourcing an appropriately qualified provider if available; any provider, documentation and responsibilities must be confirmed before acceptance.

Will CircNex erase data from our old devices?+

Data security and sanitization are project-specific. No sanitization method, certificate or guarantee is assumed. We would agree on required handling, methods, qualified service providers and documentation before taking responsibility for data-bearing assets.

Do you refurbish equipment rather than recycling everything?+

Where practical, we aim to assess condition and potential reuse before selecting an appropriate next step. Feasibility depends on device type, condition, economics and customer requirements.

Can you help with a new office or workstation refresh?+

Deployment and workstation services may include staging, monitor and dock installation, peripheral connection, hardware swaps and cable organization, subject to the agreed scope.

How can I get an estimate?+

Use the Request a Quote page to prepare your scope and contact details. During this development preview, forms do not send; an actual inquiry submission service will be connected before launch.

Is the website contact form already working?+

Not yet. Forms currently demonstrate the fields and browser validation but intentionally do not send or store messages. A secure form service must be connected and tested before the live launch.
