01 / CIRCNEX SERVICES

IT Equipment Deployment & Workstation Setup

CircNex provides professional IT equipment deployment and workstation setup services for businesses of all sizes. From receiving, staging, and unboxing equipment to installing desktops, monitors, docking stations, and peripherals, we help organizations prepare productive workspaces. Whether you are opening a new office, onboarding employees, upgrading equipment, or managing a multi-site rollout, our team provides hands-on installation and setup support.

  • Equipment receiving, staging, unboxing and deployment
  • Desktop, monitor, docking station and peripheral installation
  • Workstation cable organization, labeling and connection checks
  • New-hire setups, office moves, hardware swaps and multi-site rollouts
  • Basic device readiness checks and packaging removal

Refurbishment & Repair

This service focuses on restoring equipment, not purchasing or disposing of it. We diagnose faults, identify practical repairs and prepare suitable devices for continued use.

  • Device inspection, component testing and fault diagnosis
  • Repair recommendations and replacement of faulty parts when practical
  • Hardware cleaning, checks and preparation for redeployment
  • Readiness assessment for reuse or resale

03 / CIRCNEX SERVICES

IT Asset Retirement & Recycling

This service covers equipment at the end of its use within an organization: evaluating assets for purchase, planning their removal and choosing an appropriate next destination.

CircNex buys eligible retired IT equipment subject to assessment and agreed terms. Assets are sorted for resale, potential refurbishment or recycling; certified recycling and data destruction are coordinated through partners when included in the project scope.

  • Assessment and purchase of eligible retired IT assets
  • Equipment inventory, deinstallation and collection planning
  • Asset sorting and disposition coordination
  • Certified-partner recycling or data destruction, with agreed documentation

04 / CIRCNEX SERVICES

Network Cabling Installation & Decommissioning

CircNex supports new network cable installations and the removal of outdated low-voltage cabling. From running and organizing cables to installing patch panels and wall jacks, we help businesses build tidy, practical network connections and decommission legacy infrastructure.

  • Network cable runs, routing and organization
  • Patch panel installation, termination and labeling
  • Network wall jack installation and termination
  • Basic cable connection checks and cable management
  • Removal of retired low-voltage cabling, subject to site requirements
  • Network-rack equipment removal, racking support and site cleanup

A PARTNER OFFERING FOR MSPs & IT VENDORS

On-Site IT Field Services
& Vendor Support

CircNex acts as a local field service partner for managed service providers, technology vendors and IT companies that need someone at their customer sites in Washington, D.C., Maryland or Northern Virginia.
